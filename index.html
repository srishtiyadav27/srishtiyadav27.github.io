I am a research scholar at the Mehta Family School of Data Science and Artificial Intelligence, IIT Roorkee, under the supervision of Prof. R. Balasubramanian and Prof. Sanjeev Kumar. I am a recipient of the TCS Research Fellowship. I hold an M.Sc. in Mathematics from Banaras Hindu University, Varanasi, and a bachelor's degree from Dayalbagh Educational Institute, Agra.

Research

My primary research interest is privacy preservation in Spiking Neural Networks. My broader research interests include Differential Privacy, Federated Learning, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, and Variational Autoencoders.
