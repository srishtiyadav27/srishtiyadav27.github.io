Research

My primary research interest is privacy preservation in Spiking Neural Networks. My broader research interests include Differential Privacy, Federated Learning, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, and Variational Autoencoders.

Research Profile

My research focuses on the security and privacy of machine learning systems, spanning differential privacy, membership inference attacks, adversarial robustness, and secure training methods. Much of this work centres on spiking neural networks, an emerging architecture where security and privacy challenges are still being actively investigated. My approach combines theoretical analysis, algorithm design, and empirical evaluation to develop trustworthy and privacy-preserving machine learning systems.
